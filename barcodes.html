Example barcodes for "Scan to unpack"

Open this page on a computer. On the phone, in Pick & Sort:

  1. Tap Try an example (5 example orders).
  2. Tap 📷 Scan to unpack and point the phone at the barcodes below, one at a time.
  3. Each one shows which order it goes on. Scan the same barcode again to place the next one (move the phone away in between).
